    TDR Review — compare requirements, verify evidence, record human reviews

    Upload the conformity matrix and the supplier technical dossier (PPT / PDF / DOCX / TXT). AERIS extracts measurable targets (≤100 mA, >95 % @32°, contrast ≥400:1…), finds the matching evidence slide/page, compares the numbers, and then compares what the supplier wrote in the TDR with what they declared in the matrix (opposite OK/NOK, different figures, restated-wrong target, TDR self-conflict). Qualitative requirements need human review. Image content requires local OCR; extraction warnings are shown in the synthesis. A numeric bound is not an exact measurement, and a declared deviation is not automatically accepted.
